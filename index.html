01

Start with the risk.

Prioritize important journeys, changed behavior, and edge cases. Clarify uncertainty before treating it as an expected result.

In practice: clarifying chat behavior. I created an AI-assisted QA review workflow to cross-check acceptance criteria against Figma designs and surface missing scenarios before test-case generation. Areas of ambiguity included deactivated or deleted accounts and image/file attachments in chat. The workflow was designed to flag discrepancies and suggest edge cases for review, rather than turn assumptions into expected behavior.

02

Review what AI produces.

Check coverage, assumptions, automation candidates, and assertions. Keep test intent connected to the script.

03

Test it. Explain the result.

Validate hands-on, investigate failures, and report what the evidence establishes and what still needs checking.
